How we work

From the first conversation to the final report

  1. Brief

    We listen to what you need, who you need to reach and the constraints: dates, budget, approvals.

  2. Proposal

    A written approach with scope, team, timeline and cost. For events, a 3D preview of the set or stand.

  3. Plan

    One account lead on our side. Messages, suppliers, permits and media are agreed before anything is built.

  4. Delivery

    Our own production team runs the build and live operations, on site, wherever the event takes place.

  5. Report

    A closing report with what happened, coverage and what we would change next time.
