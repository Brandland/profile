Communication services in Mozambique, organised by the problem you need to solve

01

Reputation and stakeholders

“How do we keep the trust of government, communities and partners while the project moves forward?”

Large projects in Mozambique depend on many audiences at once: national and provincial government, local communities, business partners, employees and the press. When each of them hears a different version, trust erodes and decisions slow down. We help organisations decide what to say, to whom, in what order, and keep track of how it lands.

What we do

  • Stakeholder mapping and analysis
  • Engagement plans, key messages and question-and-answer documents
  • Institutional meetings, consultations, site visits and roundtables
  • Issues and crisis preparation, including spokesperson briefings
  • Reporting on what stakeholders say and how positions change

02

Corporate communication and PR

“How do we explain what we do to the people who matter, in a way they believe?”

A company operating in Mozambique has to speak to regulators, clients, its own staff and the media, often in Portuguese and English. We build the narrative and the messages, then put them in front of the right people through the press, internal channels and the company’s own leaders.

What we do

  • Communication strategy and annual plans
  • Corporate narrative and key messages
  • Media relations: press releases, press conferences, interviews and press visits
  • Internal communication and change communication
  • Executive communication: speeches, presentations, media training
  • Corporate identity, branding and design, when the brand needs to carry the message

03

Events and experiences

“Will this event run smoothly, and will it say what we need it to say?”

An event is a communication tool with a fixed date and no second take. We plan it around what the organisation wants its guests to think and do afterwards, then produce it with our own team. Our events range from 100 to 500 guests. The largest, the Africa50 General Shareholders Meeting 2025, ran across two venues at the same time.

What we do

  • Conferences, summits, forums, shareholder meetings and product launches
  • Own production team, with 3D previews of the set before anything is built
  • Live operations on the day: stage management, guest flow, technical crew
  • Exhibition stands for trade fairs such as FACIM and MMEC
  • Brand activations, audiovisual production, live streaming and merchandising
  • Media relations and coverage around the event

Related work

  • Africa50 General Shareholders Meeting 2025
  • M-Pesa Fintalks, Vodacom
  • ENI stand, FACIM 2024 (27 m²) and MMEC 2025
  • mcnet stand, Moztech 2025 (18 m²)
