Context

A pan-African meeting in Mozambique

Africa50 is a pan-African infrastructure investment platform whose shareholders include African governments, central banks and the African Development Bank. Its General Shareholders Meeting brings ministers, governors and senior representatives of shareholder countries together once a year.

In 2025 the meeting took place in Mozambique. Africa50 needed a local partner able to run a high-level, multi-language event with heads of delegation, protocol requirements and a programme split across two venues. [dates, names of the two venues and how Playground was appointed, to confirm with the account manager]

Challenge

Two venues, one event

Running one plenary for 500 guests is a production job. Running two venues at the same time is a coordination job: sessions have to start on time in both places, delegates and officials have to move between sites, and the content has to reach everyone in the right language.

  • Keep two venues on one schedule, with one run of show and one command structure.
  • Receive senior officials and heads of delegation according to protocol.
  • Give delegates from different countries access to every session in their working language.
  • Deliver to the standard of an international organisation, for a client based outside Mozambique.
