Who you would be working with

Playground is a communication and events agency based in Maputo, Mozambique. It has been operating for 16 years and has a team of about 30 people, including its own event production team. Playground runs more than 100 projects a year and produces corporate events from 100 to 500 guests: conferences, shareholder meetings, product launches, press conferences, delegation visits and exhibition stands. In 2025 it produced the Africa50 General Shareholders Meeting in two venues at the same time for 500 guests. Its clients include Vodacom (M-Pesa), ExxonMobil, TotalEnergies, ENI, Coral, Sasol, ABSA and UNICEF. The team works in English and Portuguese.

How we work with clients abroad

Most of our international clients see the venue for the first time a few days before the event. This is how we make that work.

One point of contact

A named project lead who answers your messages and carries the whole event from brief to wrap report.

English-speaking team

Your project lead and the people you deal with day to day work in English. On site, we handle the Portuguese.

Weekly reporting

A short written update every week: what is done, what is next, what we need from you, and the budget position.

USD or EUR invoicing

We can invoice in US dollars or euros where applicable, and in meticais for local entities. Terms are set out in the proposal.

From brief to wrap report

  1. Brief and first call

    You send the brief or book a call. We reply within 48 hours with questions and a first view on dates and venues.

  2. Proposal and budget

    A written proposal with venue options, scope, team and a detailed budget. [typical turnaround: 5 working days, to confirm]

  3. Planning

    Venue contract, suppliers, permits, protocol and media plan. 3D previews for your approval. Weekly reports start.

  4. Site visit

    Optional visit for your team, or a recorded video walk-through of the venue if you cannot travel.

  5. Event delivery

    Build, rehearsals and live operations by our own production team, with your project lead on site throughout.

  6. Wrap report

    Attendance, media coverage, photos, video and a final reconciliation of the budget.

Questions

What international organisers ask us first

Longer answers in our guide to organising a corporate event in Mozambique.

How far in advance should we contact you?

As early as you have dates. For a conference of several hundred guests, [three to four months, to confirm with the events team] gives room to secure a venue, interpretation and permits. A press conference or small launch can be organised in a few weeks. Tell us your dates and we will say honestly whether they are realistic.

Can you help our delegates with visas and permits?

Yes. We handle event permits and authorisations, coordinate with the relevant authorities, and give delegates practical guidance on entry requirements. Visa decisions belong to the Mozambican authorities, so each delegate should check the current rules for their nationality early. [confirm whether Playground issues invitation letters for delegates]

Which venues can you use?

We source venues across Maputo and beyond: conference centres, hotel ballrooms, the FACIM fairground in Marracuene for exhibitions, and outdoor or heritage spaces. We visit, compare and negotiate on your behalf. We can also run two venues at the same time, as we did for Africa50 in 2025.

Do you provide interpretation?

Yes. Simultaneous and consecutive interpretation between Portuguese and English, with booths, receivers and technical support. French and other languages can be arranged on request.

In which currency do you invoice, and how do payments work?

We can invoice international clients in US dollars or euros where applicable, and in meticais for local entities. The payment schedule and applicable taxes are set out in each proposal. [standard payment terms, deposit percentage and VAT treatment for non-resident clients to confirm with finance]

Can you work alongside our global agency or DMC?

Yes. We can contract directly with you, or act as the local production and PR team working to your agency's creative lead and brand guidelines, with one point of contact on our side.

Do you work outside Maputo?

Our base and our production team are in Maputo. We can produce events in other Mozambican cities; tell us the location and we will confirm logistics and lead times in the proposal. [list of cities where Playground has delivered events]
