About Playground

A communication consultancy that knows Mozambique from the inside

Playground has worked in the Mozambican market for 16 years. We help companies, international organisations and NGOs communicate with the people their work depends on.

Who we are

Playground is a communication consultancy based in Maputo, Mozambique.

It has been in the Mozambican market for 16 years and has a team of about 30 people.

Playground works in five areas: reputation and stakeholders, corporate communication and PR, events and experiences, content and digital, and intelligence and media monitoring.

It delivers more than 100 projects a year, with its own production team, for clients that include Vodacom, ExxonMobil, TotalEnergies, ENI, Sasol, ABSA and UNICEF.

Its largest recent event was the Africa50 General Shareholders Meeting 2025: 500 guests across two venues in Maputo at the same time.

What we believe

Organisations do better when their exchanges with the people around them are better

Our work is to improve the quality and value of the exchanges between organisations and the people around them: employees, communities, government, clients, partners and the press.

In practice, that means a few things. We start from what the other side needs to know and what it cares about, before deciding what to say. We prefer a clear message that is repeated to a clever one that is said once. And we measure whether an exchange actually happened: who came, who read, who changed their view.

Knowing Mozambique from the inside

Communication in Mozambique depends on context that is hard to read from outside: who needs to be informed before an announcement, how institutions expect to be approached, which media reach which audiences, how a provincial visit differs from a meeting in Maputo, and which suppliers can be relied on in a given week.

We have built that knowledge over 16 years of projects for companies in energy, telecoms and banking, and for international organisations. We work in Portuguese and English. [Other working languages to confirm]

For organisations arriving in the country, this is the main thing we offer: a team on the ground that can tell you what will work here, and then do it.

International partners

Working with agencies and partners from abroad

International agencies, event organisers and communication teams often need a partner in Mozambique for a single event, a launch or a media campaign. We work in three ways:

  • As the local delivery partner of an agency that keeps the client relationship
  • Directly for an organisation’s headquarters or regional office, with one point of contact in English
  • As part of a wider team, alongside the client’s own communication or events staff

Reporting is weekly and in English. Invoicing in USD or EUR is possible where applicable.
