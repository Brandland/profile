Energy · Stakeholder engagement

A stakeholder programme for a major energy project.

Mapping, engagement and reporting for a project with national visibility and local impact.

● Illustrative case. Replace with client-approved content before launch.
ClientInternational energy operator
SectorEnergy & Natural Resources
Year2024–2025
LocationMaputo and Cabo Delgado

The challenge

The project had many audiences with very different expectations: central government, provincial authorities, host communities, suppliers, media and international partners. Information was fragmented across teams and leadership lacked a single view of where relationships stood.

Our approach

“For the first time we could see, in one place, where every important relationship stood.”

Client communication lead (to confirm)

We started with a structured stakeholder map built from interviews with the client's field and corporate teams, media analysis and our own network knowledge. Each stakeholder group received a clear objective, an owner and a rhythm of engagement. We then set up a monthly intelligence brief connecting what we were hearing to the decisions leadership was preparing.

What we delivered

  • Stakeholder map and influence analysis
  • Twelve month engagement plan with owners
  • Monthly intelligence briefs for leadership
  • Daily media monitoring in Portuguese and English
  • Community information materials
