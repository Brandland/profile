Financial services · Reputation

Rebuilding media confidence for a financial institution.

Press office, spokesperson preparation and issues management over twelve months.

● Illustrative case. Replace with client-approved content before launch.
ClientCommercial bank
SectorFinancial Services
Year2023–2024
LocationMaputo

The challenge

After a period of negative coverage, the institution had become cautious with the media. Journalists found it difficult to get answers and stories were often published without the bank's view.

Our approach

We proposed a more open but well prepared approach. We agreed a small group of spokespeople, trained them, created a calendar of positive stories and set clear response times for media questions. Monitoring helped us see quickly which issues were growing.

What we delivered

  • Media relations strategy
  • Spokesperson training sessions
  • Issue and response protocol
  • Press releases and interviews
  • Monthly reputation reports
