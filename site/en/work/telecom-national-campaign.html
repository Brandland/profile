Telecom · Campaign

A national campaign that spoke to every province.

Concept, production and rollout of a multichannel campaign for a mobile operator.

● Illustrative case. Replace with client-approved content before launch.
ClientMobile operator
SectorTelecom & Technology
Year2025
LocationNational

The challenge

The brand needed a campaign that would feel relevant in Maputo and in rural districts, across age groups and languages, while supporting a specific commercial objective.

Our approach

We tested the core idea with small groups in three provinces before production. The final concept used real people and places, with versions adapted for radio, outdoor, digital and activations. Media was planned to balance national reach with local presence.

What we delivered

  • Campaign platform and key visuals
  • TV, radio and digital films
  • Outdoor and point of sale materials
  • Social media content plan
  • Field activations
