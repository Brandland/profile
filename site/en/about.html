About

Sixteen years of understanding how Mozambique communicates.

Playground was founded in Maputo in 2010. Today we are a multidisciplinary team of more than thirty people working with national and international organisations across the country.

Our story

From agency to stakeholder partner.

We started as a creative and events company. Over time our clients asked us for more: to help them understand their audiences, manage their reputation and engage communities, government and partners.

That is the direction we continue to build. Playground connects stakeholder intelligence, strategic communication and high quality execution, because in Mozambique relationships, trust and context often decide outcomes as much as the message itself.
